DJ MIX MAMUN - 10 Page Website

Pages:
1. index.html - Home
2. videos.html - Latest 10 videos (each video has its own embedded player)
3. dj-mix.html - DJ Mix Zone
4. remix.html - Remix Zone
5. popular.html - Popular Music
6. about.html - About
7. gallery.html - Graphic gallery
8. updates.html - Channel updates
9. contact.html - Contact
10. subscribe.html - Subscribe

How to use:
- Extract the ZIP file.
- Keep the assets folder beside all HTML files.
- Open index.html in a browser to preview.
- Upload the complete folder contents to your hosting/public_html directory.
- The latest-video feed uses the public YouTube RSS feed through RSS2JSON. If that third-party service is unavailable, use the YouTube links shown on the page.
- For best compatibility, host the site online (some browsers restrict requests from local file:// pages).
